Accessibility Statement

SACCO is committed to making its website accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability.

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. This website is being reviewed and improved as its content and services develop.

Measures currently in place

  • Semantic page structure and descriptive page titles.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Keyboard-accessible links with visible focus indicators.
  • High-contrast text and controls.
  • Reduced or disabled animation when the device requests reduced motion.

Feedback and assistance

Should you experience an accessibility barrier, please contact SACCO through @sacco.us . Please identify the page and describe the difficulty encountered. A dedicated accessibility email address will be added when the official SACCO domain and email service are activated.
